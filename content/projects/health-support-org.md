<section class="cs-section"><h2>The challenge</h2><p>The platform was growing fast but had no formal support function — engineers fielded customer issues ad hoc, response times were unpredictable, and there was no way to measure or improve service quality.</p><p>In a regulated health context, that was a liability. The company needed a structured, auditable support operation that could scale and hold to defined service levels.</p></section>
<section class="cs-section"><h2>Approach</h2><div class="cs-phases"><div class="cs-phase"><div class="cs-phase-num">01</div><div class="cs-phase-body"><h3>Operating model</h3><p>Defined the support operating model on ITIL 4 — incident, request, and problem management with clear ownership and guiding principles.</p></div></div><div class="cs-phase"><div class="cs-phase-num">02</div><div class="cs-phase-body"><h3>Tiering &amp; SLAs</h3><p>Designed a four-tier model (L1–L3 + engineering escalation) with priority definitions and response/resolution SLAs per tier.</p></div></div><div class="cs-phase"><div class="cs-phase-num">03</div><div class="cs-phase-body"><h3>Tooling &amp; knowledge</h3><p>Configured the service desk, queues, and a knowledge base so L1 could resolve the long tail without escalating.</p></div></div><div class="cs-phase"><div class="cs-phase-num">04</div><div class="cs-phase-body"><h3>Hiring &amp; ramp</h3><p>Built the hiring plan, role definitions, and onboarding path, then ramped the first cohort against live volume.</p></div></div><div class="cs-phase"><div class="cs-phase-num">05</div><div class="cs-phase-body"><h3>Continual improvement</h3><p>Instrumented dashboards and a review cadence to feed recurring issues back into product and knowledge.</p></div></div></div></section>
<section class="cs-section"><h2>How it fit together</h2><div class="cs-arch"><div class="cs-arch-row"><div class="cs-arch-tag">Incident mgmt</div><p>Structured intake, prioritization, and resolution with defined SLAs per severity.</p></div><div class="cs-arch-row"><div class="cs-arch-tag">Escalation</div><p>Clear L1→L2→L3→engineering paths with ownership and handoff criteria at each tier.</p></div><div class="cs-arch-row"><div class="cs-arch-tag">Knowledge</div><p>A maintained knowledge base that powers first-contact resolution and onboarding.</p></div><div class="cs-arch-row"><div class="cs-arch-tag">Measurement</div><p>Dashboards for SLA attainment, FCR, backlog, and recurring-issue trends.</p></div></div><div class="cs-diagram">Architecture / flow diagram — to add</div></section>
<section class="cs-section"><h2>Outcomes</h2><ul class="cs-outcomes"><li>Went from no coverage to a 24/7 operation with defined SLAs.</li><li>First-contact resolution climbed toward the 90% target as knowledge matured.</li><li>P1 incidents acknowledged in under two hours, consistently and measurably.</li><li>Recurring issues now feed a structured problem-management loop instead of disappearing.</li></ul></section>

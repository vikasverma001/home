<section class="cs-section"><h2>The challenge</h2><p>Years of unmanaged growth left the JIRA Cloud instance with dozens of bespoke workflows, inconsistent permission schemes, and one-off automations nobody fully understood. Cross-project reporting was meaningless and onboarding was slow.</p><p>The delivery org needed standardization without grinding active projects to a halt.</p></section>
<section class="cs-section"><h2>Approach</h2><div class="cs-phases"><div class="cs-phase"><div class="cs-phase-num">01</div><div class="cs-phase-body"><h3>Audit</h3><p>Inventoried every workflow, scheme, custom field, and automation rule to understand the real surface area.</p></div></div><div class="cs-phase"><div class="cs-phase-num">02</div><div class="cs-phase-body"><h3>Standard workflows</h3><p>Designed a small set of standard, reusable workflows to replace the dozen near-duplicates.</p></div></div><div class="cs-phase"><div class="cs-phase-num">03</div><div class="cs-phase-body"><h3>Permission &amp; scheme cleanup</h3><p>Consolidated permission and notification schemes into a maintainable, role-based set.</p></div></div><div class="cs-phase"><div class="cs-phase-num">04</div><div class="cs-phase-body"><h3>Automation rationalization</h3><p>Rebuilt automation rules around the standard workflows, removing redundant and conflicting ones.</p></div></div><div class="cs-phase"><div class="cs-phase-num">05</div><div class="cs-phase-body"><h3>Governance &amp; handoff</h3><p>Documented the governance model and trained admins so the instance stays clean over time.</p></div></div></div></section>
<section class="cs-section"><h2>How it fit together</h2><div class="cs-arch"><div class="cs-arch-row"><div class="cs-arch-tag">Standard workflows</div><p>A small reusable set of workflows replaces dozens of bespoke ones.</p></div><div class="cs-arch-row"><div class="cs-arch-tag">Schemes</div><p>Consolidated, role-based permission and notification schemes.</p></div><div class="cs-arch-row"><div class="cs-arch-tag">Automation</div><p>Rationalized automation rules aligned to the standard workflows.</p></div><div class="cs-arch-row"><div class="cs-arch-tag">Governance</div><p>A documented model and admin training keep the instance maintainable.</p></div></div><div class="cs-diagram">Architecture / flow diagram — to add</div></section>
<section class="cs-section"><h2>Outcomes</h2><ul class="cs-outcomes"><li>Twelve overlapping workflows consolidated into a standard reusable set.</li><li>40+ automation rules rebuilt around the new model, removing conflicts.</li><li>Cross-project reporting became meaningful for the first time.</li><li>New-project setup and onboarding dropped from days to hours.</li></ul></section>

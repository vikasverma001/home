Most product managers live in JIRA every day — and almost none of them can actually configure it. That gap is bigger than it looks. The PM who understands workflow schemes, automation rules, and permission models can shape how an entire organization delivers, instead of just filing tickets into someone else's structure.

## Why configuration is a product skill

A board is a model of how your team thinks work flows. When that model is wrong, every standup pays the tax.

## Workflow design

The defaults are rarely right. A few well-placed statuses and transitions remove far more friction than another status meeting.

## Automation that earns its keep

The rules worth building are the ones that remove repetitive human steps — auto-assignment, transition triggers, and SLA nudges.
